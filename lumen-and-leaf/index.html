Lumen & Leaf

Bend the sunlight. Bring the old greenhouses back to bloom.

Coming soon to Google Play

Free to try. One purchase unlocks the full game. No ads, ever.

Lumen & Leaf is a calm, handcrafted light puzzle game for Android phones. Drag pieces onto a potting bench, turn and flip them, and guide the morning sun to every plant that needs it, while the shade-loving ferns stay in the dark. The light follows every move at once, so you can try an idea and see what happens straight away.

What's inside

  • Real optics, made playful: mirrors reflect light, glass wedges bend it by one exact step, and prisms split white light into red, yellow and blue. Color filters join them too.
  • 40 handcrafted puzzles in three greenhouses: the Herb House, the Fern Grotto and the Rose Garden. Each one teaches you a single new idea.
  • Free hints, as many as you like, whenever you are stuck. Solve a level without one to plant its rare flower.
  • No timers, no energy, no currency. Play at your own pace.
  • Made for everyone: colorblind shape markers, a reduced motion setting, larger text, and vibration and sound effects you can turn off.
  • Play in English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese or Traditional Chinese.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account: your progress stays on your phone.

Free to try

The eight Herb House levels and the first four Fern Grotto levels are free. One purchase opens the rest of the estate, and every greenhouse still to come.

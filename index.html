Lumen & Leaf is a calm, handcrafted light puzzle game. Drag pieces onto a potting bench, turn and flip them, and guide the morning sun to every plant that needs it, while the shade-loving ferns stay in the dark. The light follows every move at once, so you can try an idea and see what happens straight away.

In the game

  • Mirrors reflect light, glass wedges bend it by one exact step, and prisms split white light into red, yellow and blue. Color filters join them too.
  • 40 handcrafted puzzles in three greenhouses: the Herb House, the Fern Grotto and the Rose Garden.
  • Free hints, as many as you like. Solve a level without one to plant its rare flower.
  • No timers, no energy, no currency.
  • Colorblind shape markers, reduced motion, larger text, and vibration and sound you can turn off.
  • English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese and Traditional Chinese.
  • No ads, no tracking, no account: your progress stays on your phone.

The eight Herb House levels and the first four Fern Grotto levels are free. One purchase opens the rest of the estate, and every greenhouse still to come.

Lumen & Leaf is a calm light puzzle game. Drag mirrors, glass and prisms onto a potting bench, turn and flip them, and guide the morning sun to every plant that needs it, while the shade-loving ferns stay in the dark. The light follows every move at once, so you can try an idea and see what happens straight away.
